Methodology: state-level PM2.5 uses each state/UT's capital or largest CPCB-monitored city as a proxy, since no single public source consolidates a true state-wide network average. NCAP non-attainment city counts are from published NCAP programme reviews (Maharashtra highest at 19, Uttar Pradesh 17); Goa, Kerala, Manipur, Mizoram and Tripura are confirmed not on the NCAP non-attainment list.

Compiled dataset for educational analysis. Sources: CPCB CAAQM/NAQI, US EPA AirNow embassy monitors, IQAir World Air Quality Report, UrbanEmissions.info. Interpolated years are flagged in the Source column.
